A real lineage: run B, seed 1 — one of 3 of 10 pre-registered seed runs where evolution held (verdict: no; see backup)

Talking points: this is one real family from the run, not an illustration. Each generation the swarm buys 50 more DFT labels and breeds children from them. A child must not be worse than its parent on held-out labels (forces and reaction energies) or it is stillborn; near-copies are rejected because copies cannot disagree. Survivors are scored by their marginal contribution to the swarm's served answers, and anything that adds nothing is pruned, including the AIMNet2 founder and the child that replaced it. Its own child survives with the highest fitness in the swarm. Breeding is the experimental part: under the pre-registered test it did not beat the static swarm (it held in 3 of 10 seed runs; backup slide). The result on the next slide is the static swarm.
