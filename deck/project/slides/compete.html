04 · Let them grow

Growing the swarm beats static. Our selection didn't.

pre-registered · 12 new seeds · 800 DFT labels · three model families

Grow: each round, fine-tune a niche specialist per family → keep parents and base models → serve the best answer.

Δ force RMSE vs static (eV/Å) · dot = seed, filled = significant · ◆ mean ± 95% t-CI over seeds · results/evolution_e

Talking points: run E was pre-registered after exploratory runs hinted that growing works better than selecting; it used 12 new seeds, three model families and the same 800-label DFT budget for every arm. Growing the swarm, adding one fine-tuned niche specialist per family each round while never removing the base models, lowered served force error versus the static swarm in 10 of 12 seeds and was worse in none: on average −0.018 eV/Å, 95% interval over seeds −0.024 to −0.013, with lower reaction-energy error too (−0.026 eV) and no loss of calibration. The arm that matches our original flagship rule, with a gate and fitness pruning, gained nothing on average (−0.001, interval −0.017 to +0.016) and was worse than static in 4 of 12 seeds. The honest lesson: variation helps, our selection didn't. The live service still runs the static swarm.
