03 · The signal

Disagreement is free data. Independent models also get its size right.

Copies of one model

36×

Four LoRA seeds of MACE rank errors well but under-report them: their spread is 36× smaller than their error (AIMNet2 seeds: 2.4×).

Three independent models

0.92

Their spread flags where AIMNet2 is wrong (AUROC 0.917) on 1,485 held-out structures.

Talking points: when models are genuinely different, their disagreement is an error signal that costs no labels. MACE, AIMNet2 and Orb have different architectures and training sets; their spread ranks AIMNet2's failures at AUROC 0.92. Fine-tuned copies of one model share its mistakes: four LoRA seeds of MACE disagree 36 times less than they are wrong. So diversity in the population is not decoration; it is the sensor.
