01 · The problem

Intelligence is abundant.
Ground truth is not.

Three independent models answered all 5,632 structures. The swarm could afford 200 DFT labels.

Each dot: one Transition1x structure in this study · orange: one DFT label (the budget per arm, 3.6%)

Talking points: machine-learned potentials made predictions nearly free; the reference calculation (DFT) and lab time did not get cheaper. In this study three foundation models with different architectures and training sets predicted every structure, and every arm of the experiment got the same 200 DFT labels. The question is where to spend them, and which answers to trust without one. Labels are Transition1x lookups, not live DFT.
