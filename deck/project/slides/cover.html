Fittest

Survival of the fittest, in materials science.

Pavel Tkachyk

github.com/Pavel-Tk/fittest

Talking points: Fittest is a swarm of machine-learned interatomic potentials competing for a fixed DFT budget. DFT goes where the models disagree; models that add something to the swarm survive; offspring are bred on the DFT labels and must pass a gate. It runs on the NVIDIA ALCHEMI Toolkit and anyone can call it through one MCP server. Breeding with selection failed its pre-registered test; growing the swarm without selection passed a separate pre-registered test on 12 new seeds (run E); the live service runs the static swarm.
