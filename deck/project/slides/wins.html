The honest reason: from those labels the Calibrator discovered that the AIMNet2 + Orb average is the answer to serve; fixing that average in hindsight scores the same (0.202). It trusts 71 percent of answers and none of the 96 badly wrong ones; calibration (ECE 0.08) comes from the Skeptic's random audits.
