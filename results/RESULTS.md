# TrustSwarm — results

<!-- SUMMARY:START -->
**TrustSwarm on Transition1x** — leak-free split, 200 Transition1x label lookups per arm (40 per round × 5 rounds; no live DFT); B′ additionally uses MACE LoRA seeds fine-tuned on 150 of arm D's labels; 95% reaction-bootstrap CIs.

* **Better served answers, by discovery:** TrustSwarm's served force RMSE on held-out eval is **0.206 eV/Å** vs 0.286–0.317 for the baselines (wrong on 40% of eval frames vs 54%–68%). It served `mean(aimnet2+orb)` on 1373/1485 eval frames: the gain comes from discovering that combination from 200 oracle labels; no baseline had subset candidates. A fixed AIMNet2+Orb average chosen in hindsight (using eval labels) scores 0.202 eV/Å — as good.
* **Calibrated trust:** ECE 0.080, Brier 0.185; significantly better than A_single, A_random, B_seed, B_mace_seeds, C_collective; tied with C_audit — the gain comes from the Skeptic's random audits of trusted answers.
* **Negative result — error ranking:** TrustSwarm's AUROC (eval 0.730) is significantly worse than A_single, B_seed, B_mace_seeds, C_collective, C_audit. Best ranker: B_mace_seeds (0.853).
* **Committee disagreement detects AIMNet2's failures best:** AUROC vs 'AIMNet2 wrong' 0.917 (C_collective).
* **Seed ensembles rank but do not measure:** B′ AUROC 0.853 (best); B ties C at 0.802; on all 3,157 test frames at 0.4 eV/Å the AIMNet2 seed spread beats the committee (0.905 vs 0.866) — but seed spreads are 2.4–30× smaller than the errors on the 1,485 eval frames: not usable as an error bar.
* **Silent failures:** TrustSwarm has 0/96 on eval (badly wrong, F-RMSE > 0.4) and 1/115 on pool; not significantly different from 5 of 5 non-random baselines, 4 of which trust nothing (zero by construction).
* **Planted failures:** 0.45 caught vs 0.33–0.70; significantly better than C_collective, not significant vs 5 of 6.
* **Self-repair:** the Curator chose and LoRA-fine-tuned `mace_mpa0`; not shipped — forces 0.878 → 0.500 eV/Å but relative-energy MAE 0.402 → 0.414 eV on 36 held-out labels.
* **Leakage controlled:** split by reactant- and product-linked components; 0 shared endpoint geometries (a per-reaction split had 732 near-duplicate pool/eval endpoints).
* Details, history and failures: `results/RESULTS.md`.

  * `holdout_rel_energy_mae_not_worse` (trustswarm): 0.414 <= 0.402 → **fail**

* Leak check, hold-out vs training (component-disjoint): not recorded in the fine-tune metadata.
* Our gate requires every check to pass; the fine-tune is **not shipped** even though the toolkit's own acceptance passes.

## Splits and leakage

* pool/eval: split by reactant- and product-linked components (reactions sharing any endpoint geometry are linked): 37 components over 287 reactions; frames per split: eval 1485, planted 1254, pool 1672, skeptic_ref 1221.
* skeptic_ref/planted: split by formula, components kept whole (0 reactions moved).
* Shared endpoint geometries after the split: pool|eval: 0, skeptic_ref|planted: 0; near-duplicates (max|ΔR| < 1e-3 Å): pool|eval: 0, skeptic_ref|planted: 0 (per-reaction split: 732 pool/eval near-duplicates).
* Labels reach agents only through `oracle_result` events (tested); eval labels are never revealed; replayed fine-tunes must match this run's labels and share no uid or geometry with held-out frames.

## Failures and caveats (reported, not hidden)

* D_trustswarm: fine-tune not shipped (acceptance verdict 'fail' != 'pass')
* TrustSwarm's error ranking (AUROC eval) is significantly worse than: A_single, B_seed, B_mace_seeds, C_collective, C_audit.
* The Calibrator's threshold τ never left its floor (0.620): flags were set by the round-0 70th-percentile rule, not by learning.
* Reputations ended nearly equal (0.86–0.89): reputation-weighted bidding behaved like plain summed bids.
* TrustSwarm's served-answer gain equals a fixed AIMNet2+Orb average chosen in hindsight (0.202 eV/Å).
* The Calibrator's labels are mostly bid-won (high σ); audits reduce but do not remove this selection bias.
* The synthetic smoke test (`scripts/make_synthetic.py`) shows the pipeline runs; it is not evidence for any claim.
