# TrustSwarm — results

<!-- SUMMARY:START -->
**TrustSwarm on Transition1x (leak-free split, 40×5 = 200 oracle calls per arm, 95% reaction-bootstrap CIs).**

* **Better served answers:** TrustSwarm's served force RMSE on held-out eval is **0.206 eV/Å** vs 0.286–0.317 for every baseline; wrong on 40% of eval frames vs 54%–68%. It learned to serve the subset mean of the models that is most often right.
* **Calibrated trust:** ECE 0.080, Brier 0.185; significantly better than A_single, A_random, B_seed, B_mace_seeds, C_collective, tied with C_audit — the gain comes from the Skeptic's random audits of trusted answers.
* **Negative result — error ranking:** TrustSwarm's AUROC (eval 0.730) is significantly worse than A_single, B_seed, B_mace_seeds, C_collective, C_audit. Best ranker on eval: B_mace_seeds (0.853).
* **Heterogeneous disagreement detects AIMNet2's failures best:** AUROC vs 'AIMNet2 wrong' 0.917 (C_collective).
* **Recall@K:** TrustSwarm 0.186; significantly better than A_random.
* **Ranking ≠ calibration:** on eval the median seed spread is MACE LoRA seeds 0.009 vs median error 0.266 eV/Å (×30 under-dispersed); AIMNet2 ensemble members 0.109 vs median error 0.257 eV/Å (×2 under-dispersed).
* **Self-repair:** the Curator fine-tuned `mace_mpa0` on its oracle labels; not shipped — acceptance verdict 'fail' != 'pass'.
* **Leakage controlled:** pool/eval and skeptic_ref/planted share 0 endpoint geometries (Transition1x reuses reactants; the naive split shared 732 near-duplicates).
* Full tables, per-version history and failures: `results/RESULTS.md`.

* Held-out slice of the oracle labels (36 frames, never trained on): force RMSE 0.878 → 0.500 eV/Å, relative-energy MAE 0.402 → 0.414 eV.
  * check `from_scratch_ratio` (alchemi): 0.773 <= 1.0 → pass
  * check `alchemi_report_accepted` (alchemi): pass
  * check `holdout_force_rmse_not_worse` (trustswarm): 0.500 <= 0.878 → pass
  * check `holdout_rel_energy_mae_not_worse` (trustswarm): 0.414 <= 0.402 → **fail**
* Geometry leak check of the training labels against eval: 0 matches → passed.
* Our gate requires every check to pass, so the fine-tune is **not shipped** even though the toolkit's own acceptance (True) passes: it fixes forces but makes relative energies slightly worse.

## Splits and leakage

* pool/eval by connected component of shared reactant/product geometry: 37 components over 287 reactions; frames per split: eval 1485, planted 1254, pool 1672, skeptic_ref 1221.
* skeptic_ref/planted by formula, components kept whole (0 reactions moved).
* Shared endpoint geometries after the split: pool|eval: 0, skeptic_ref|planted: 0; near-duplicates (max|ΔR| < 1e-3 Å): pool|eval: 0, skeptic_ref|planted: 0.
* Labels reach agents only through `oracle_result` events (tested); eval labels are never revealed; replayed fine-tunes must match this run's labels and share no uid or geometry with held-out frames.

## Failures and caveats (reported, not hidden)

* D_trustswarm: fine-tune not shipped (acceptance verdict 'fail' != 'pass')
* D's error ranking (AUROC eval) is significantly worse than: A_single, B_seed, B_mace_seeds, C_collective, C_audit.
* The Calibrator's labels are mostly bid-won (high σ); audits reduce but do not remove this selection bias.
* The synthetic smoke test (`scripts/make_synthetic.py`) shows the pipeline runs; it is not evidence for any claim.
